# Continuity ledger — What the Almanac Rounded (authoring instrument, not manuscript prose)

## Chronology (fiction calendar; weekdays fixed: 6 April is a Thursday)
- Almanac installed 22 years before the story. Ruben Reader years 1–8.
- Bell Year: year 5 (17 years before). Fourteen percent given 9 December; lender reviews;
  thirty-one households leave in seven weeks, first (Pearson) in the first week of February,
  last on 31 March (the Harlans). Dellen (46) was 29 then, a young man behind the store counter.
  Odd's drawer labels carry two-digit years ('04 to '65); no century is given, so weekdays follow
  the fiction calendar above, not a real one.
- Bell Resolution: that winter, 7–2 (Ruben and Odd dissent). Courage 0.04 added: a fraction, applied as
  four points to the PRINTED table (and so to what was read aloud); Article 14 footer carries the
  truth, written the same week. Margin lasts about two years. After entry 884 table = footer; the
  only rounding is Tamsin's spoken one (mean +3.1 over 14 years).
- Entry 884: Ruben zeroes courage in February of year 8, writes "ok", tells Odd, dies three
  weeks later in March (14 years ago). The note to Tamsin is in the drawer; she first read it the
  morning after the funeral and put it back. Ledger: 1,162 entries; 884 are Ruben's (883 with a
  written reason), the rest the Almanac's own seasonal calibrations.
- Tamsin Reader 14 years; Hettie secretary 15; Greta chair 9; Ilka (36) was 18 in the Bell Year.

## Numbers
- 212 households; two-thirds = 142. Dellen's count 118/61/33. Vote: ballots 197, sale 128–69
  (fails), lease 161–36 (carries). Twenty-three walk out on 17 Aug.
- Hives: 110 in April, 9 lost 30 June, 101 after. Hive 3 row 2 counts (in/out per 30 s): 71/62 Apr,
  ~50 Jul, 40/34 5 Oct, 22/19 19 Oct. Gauges 63 then 112 (made in the evenings after Joss's day off).
- Reservoir: 61% 6 Apr, 58% 4 May, 41% projected 8 Jul, 34% 17 Aug, below intake 2 Sep,
  29% 16 Sep, 26% 5 Oct, 28% after rain (two points).
- Weekly rain chance: 31% [18–46] 6 Apr; 22% 4 May; 26% 15 Jun (read as "about a quarter");
  4% [2–9] 17 Aug; 6% 16 Sep; 5% 28 Sep; 7% [4–12] 5 Oct; 9% [5–15] 19 Oct; 11% 26 Oct.
- The rain of 19–20 Oct: basin mean 6.4 mm, 9.2 at Odd's, 11.2 at the Fenns'. The nine percent was
  for a 12 mm basin-average event, so the named event did NOT occur; the Almanac says so at 04:07
  and Tamsin's Reader's Note records it. 157 days since the 16 May rain.
- Season chance of a normal year: 12% [4–24] (Apr–Jun; "better than one in five" sits inside it)
  then 9% [3–17] (17 Aug). The two intervals belong to different sheets.
- Offer: $58 million; smallest household about $60,000, mean about $270,000.
